Privacy Policy for Antigravity Mobile

Effective Date: August 29, 2026

This Privacy Policy explains how Antigravity Mobile ("we", "our", or "the Application") collects, uses, and protects information when you use our mobile application and related services.

1. Information We Collect

We believe in data minimization. We do not require you to create an account with us to use the app, and we do not store your private code or remote workspace credentials on our servers.

Local Device Data: Your remote workspace URLs, connection tokens, and display preferences are stored exclusively on your device using encrypted local storage. They are never transmitted to our servers.

However, the Application integrates third-party SDKs to provide essential platform capabilities and monetization:

  • Google Play Services: Core platform libraries and security integrity services.
  • Google AdMob: Advertisements shown within the app. AdMob may collect and process device identifiers, including the Google Advertising ID (AAID), IP address, coarse location, and app interaction data to serve personalized or contextual advertisements and measure ad performance.

2. Device Permissions Required

The Application requests the following standard Android permissions strictly to fulfill its features:

  • INTERNET & ACCESS_NETWORK_STATE: Required to connect to your remote workspace environment and load live session content.
  • POST_NOTIFICATIONS: On Android 13 (API 33) and newer, used to deliver background task notifications, terminal alerts, and tool approval requests.
  • VIBRATE: Used for tactile haptic feedback and high-priority alerts.

3. Third-Party Services & Privacy Policies

Our advertising and service partners operate under their respective privacy policies:

  • Google Privacy & Terms
  • Google AdMob Data Safety & Policy Guidelines

4. User Rights & Opt-Outs

You have full control over advertising and tracking identifiers:

  • Reset or Delete Advertising ID: You can reset or delete your Android Advertising ID at any time via Settings > Google > Ads > Reset/Delete advertising ID.
  • Ad-Free Mode: You can toggle ads off directly within the app settings.
  • Data Deletion: Clearing application storage via Android Settings will permanently purge all cached sessions, URLs, and local settings.

5. Data Security & Encryption

All network communications between the Application, Google Antigravity servers, and third-party APIs are encrypted in transit using industry-standard Transport Layer Security (TLS/HTTPS). Cleartext HTTP traffic is blocked by default.

6. Children's Privacy

The Application is intended for developers, technical professionals, and general audiences aged 13 and above. We do not knowingly collect personal identifiable information from children under 13.
